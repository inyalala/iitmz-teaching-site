In-Class Assignments

Seven graded assignments, completed during lab sessions on alternating weeks. Each assignment is worth approximately 1.4% of your final grade (10% total). MTech assignments include an additional industry-design section absent from the BS version. Assignments are due at the end of the lab session.

Availability: Assignment sheets are released at the start of each lab session and will appear below when active. Completed assignments remain here for revision.
#TopicWeekSheet
A1 OOP: Class hierarchy, encapsulation, operator overloading + system design Week 2 Open
A2 Arrays: Rotate, two-sum, amortized analysis + sparse matrix design Week 4 Released in lab
A3 Stacks, Queues, Linked Lists: MinStack, queue-from-stacks, cycle detection + LRU cache design Week 6 Released in lab
A4 Sorting and Trees: MergeSort, HeapSort, BST delete, B-tree trace + database index design Week 8 Released in lab
A5 DP and Greedy: Knapsack, coin change, Huffman coding + supply chain optimization Week 10 Released in lab
A6 Graph Algorithms: BFS, Dijkstra, Floyd-Warshall on given networks + network capacity design Week 12 Released in lab
A7 Comprehensive: Randomized algorithms, backtracking, algorithm design under constraints + parallel algorithm design Week 14 Released in lab

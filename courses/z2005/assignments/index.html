In-Class Assignments

Seven graded assignments, completed during lab sessions on alternating weeks. Each assignment is worth approximately 1.4% of your final grade (10% total). Assignments are due at the end of the lab session — no extensions are granted.

Availability: Assignment sheets are released at the start of each lab session and will appear in the table below when active. Completed assignments are retained here for revision reference.
#TopicWeekSheet
A1 OOP: Design and implement a class hierarchy with encapsulation and polymorphism Week 2 Open
A2 Arrays and Dynamic Arrays: Implement rotate, two-sum, and amortized analysis Week 4 Released in lab
A3 Stacks, Queues, and Linked Lists: Implement MinStack, queue-from-stacks, and cycle detection Week 6 Released in lab
A4 Sorting and Trees: Implement MergeSort, HeapSort, and BST delete Week 8 Released in lab
A5 Dynamic Programming and Greedy: Knapsack, coin change, and Huffman coding Week 10 Released in lab
A6 Graph Algorithms: BFS shortest path, Dijkstra, and Floyd-Warshall on given networks Week 12 Released in lab
A7 Comprehensive: Randomized algorithms, backtracking, and algorithm design under constraints Week 14 Released in lab
